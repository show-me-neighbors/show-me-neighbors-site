It covers your use of the Show-Me Neighbors website, app and phone line (together, the "Platform") to book companion visits, rides and errands. "You" means the person who makes the family account. The "client" means the person who gets the help, which may be you or a loved one. Please read it carefully and keep a copy. You can always find the current version at showmeneighbors.com/terms-of-use.html.

By checking the box when you make your account, you agree to everything in this Agreement. If you do not agree, please do not make an account or book visits.

If there is a medical emergency, call 911 right away. Show-Me Neighbors is not an emergency service.

1. Who can make an account

You must be at least 18 years old. Everything you tell us must be true and kept up to date, including the client's address, health and safety notes, and emergency contacts. If you book for someone else, you confirm that they know about the visits and are okay with them, or that you have the legal right to arrange help for them. You must be able to make a legal agreement, follow the law when using the Platform, and show proof of who you are if we reasonably ask.

2. Independent Neighbors and families

Show-Me Neighbors is a platform. It connects families with local Neighbors who offer companionship and everyday help. Neighbors are independent contractors, not employees of Show-Me Neighbors. Neighbors choose their own hours and which visits they take. Show-Me Neighbors does not give the help itself and does not supervise each visit.

Every Neighbor passes a criminal background check across all 50 states, a driving record check and a live video interview, and finishes our onboarding course before their first visit. Checks are repeated at least once a year. Background checks lower risk, but no check can promise how a person will act. To the fullest extent the law allows, Show-Me Neighbors is not responsible for what a Neighbor or client does or fails to do, including damage to property or injury. We do not stand behind advice or information a Neighbor gives, and you decide for yourself how much to rely on it.

We are not a home care agency, a home health agency or a medical provider. Neighbors do not give personal care or medical care. If the client needs that kind of help, please use a licensed home care or health care provider. We are happy to tell you honestly if we think a Neighbor is not the right fit.

3. What Neighbors do and never do

Neighbors offer conversation and company, rides, errands, shopping, going along to appointments, light help around the house, simple meals, and help with phones and technology.

Neighbors never:

  • give hands-on personal care, such as bathing, dressing, grooming, brushing teeth, help in the bathroom, or feeding;
  • give, sort, or remind about medicine, or do any medical or nursing task, such as wound care;
  • lift or carry a person, or help someone walk, stand, or move from a bed, chair, car or the floor;
  • do deep cleaning, yard work, or jobs that need ladders, power tools or heavy lifting;
  • handle the client's money, cards, checks, passwords, bills, will or property.

Please do not ask a Neighbor to do anything on this list. If you do, they will kindly say no and let us know.

4. Someone is home for every visit

The client must be home for every visit, and every visit includes some time together. A Neighbor can run errands as part of a visit, but will never go to an empty house. Neighbors drive their own cars and do not have wheelchair vans. For rides, the client must be able to get in and out of the car on their own. A walker or folding wheelchair is fine if the client can do that.

When the Neighbor arrives, the client gives them the short visit code shown in the app. This is how we know someone was home and the visit really started. The Neighbor's app also records their location when they check in and check out.

5. Booking visits

  • You book in the app or online. Visits are offered to your favorite Neighbors first, then to other Neighbors nearby. The first one to say yes gets the visit.
  • We do our best, but we cannot promise a visit will be filled, that it can happen the same day, or that the same Neighbor will come every time.
  • If no Neighbor has said yes about 12 hours before the visit, we let you know and the visit is released at no charge.
  • Tell us in the app about pets in the home and if you would prefer a man or a woman, and we only offer your visits to Neighbors who fit.

6. Prices and payment

  • Hourly price. $34 an hour on weekdays from 8 am to 6 pm, and $38 an hour on weekday evenings after 6 pm and on Saturdays and Sundays. Current prices are always shown in the app before you book.
  • Minimum. Each visit is at least one hour. After that, time is billed by the 5 minutes, from check-in to check-out.
  • Mileage. Miles driven for the client during the visit, such as rides and errands, are added at the IRS business mileage rate (76 cents a mile in 2026). 100% of it goes to the Neighbor. We never charge for the Neighbor's drive to and from the client's home.
  • When you pay. Your card on file is charged after each visit, and you get a receipt by email. If you have plan hours, an employer benefit or gift credit, the app uses those first.
  • If a charge does not go through. We will try again and let you know. We may pause new bookings until the amount is paid.
  • Questions about a charge. Tell us within 30 days of the visit and we will look into it.
  • Refunds. Charges for finished visits and late cancellations are not refunded, unless we made a billing mistake.
  • Price changes. We may change prices, plans and promotions. New prices show in the app before you book and never apply to a visit you already booked.
  • Private pay only. We do not bill Medicare, Medicaid or other insurance.

7. Changing or cancelling

  • Change or cancel up to 24 hours before a visit at no charge.
  • If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, one hour at that visit's price is charged, because the Neighbor set that time aside for you.
  • If a Neighbor cancels or does not come, you are not charged, and we will let you know right away.

8. Monthly plans, gift cards and employer codes

You can prepay a monthly plan of visit hours, use a gift card, or use a code from an employer. Plan prices and what each plan includes are shown in the app. You can cancel a monthly plan any time in the app or by emailing us. It ends right away and you are not charged for the next month. Plan payments already made are not refunded. If we close an account because this Agreement was broken, we may keep unused plan hours or credit.

9. No tipping, gifts or money

Show-Me Neighbors is a no-tipping service. Please do not give Neighbors tips, cash, checks, gift cards, loans or gifts. A small thank-you treat is fine. Please never give a Neighbor the client's cards, PINs or passwords, or ask them to sign anything or get involved in the client's money or property.

10. Safety and emergencies

  • If the client falls, is hurt, or seems very unwell, the Neighbor will not lift them. They will call 911 first, stay with the client until help arrives, and then contact you and us.
  • Please add at least one emergency contact in the app and keep it up to date.
  • Please tell us about anything a Neighbor should know to keep the client and themselves safe, such as memory loss, falls, pets, or anything about the home.
  • A Neighbor may end a visit early if they feel unsafe. You are charged only for the time they were there.
  • Neighbors are not there to watch over the client after the visit ends, and are not a replacement for 24-hour care or a medical alert service.

11. Treating Neighbors with respect

You agree that you, the client and anyone else in the home will treat Neighbors with respect. Harassment, threats, unsafe behavior or discrimination of any kind are not allowed. If that happens, we may end the visit and close the account.

12. Book through us

Neighbors you meet through Show-Me Neighbors are booked through the Platform. While you have an account, and for 12 months after your last visit with a Neighbor, you agree not to hire or pay that Neighbor directly, or through anyone else, for the kinds of help we offer. This keeps prices fair, keeps background checks current, and keeps everyone protected.

13. Visit notes, ratings and privacy

We share with the Neighbor only what they need for the visit, such as the client's first name, address, the help wanted and safety notes. After each visit the Neighbor writes a short note that you and the contacts you choose can read. You can rate each visit from 1 to 5 stars. Ratings go to our office, not the Neighbor. Our privacy policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Message and data rates may apply. You can stop marketing messages any time, but we still need to send messages about booked visits and charges.
