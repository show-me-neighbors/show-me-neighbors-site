About us

We want to be part of the solution

A quarter of American adults are now in the "sandwich generation": raising kids, or helping grown ones, while looking after a parent who is 65 or older. For Americans in their 40s, it is more than half.1 Sixty-three million Americans are family caregivers, and 6 in 10 of them hold a job, too.2 And here at home, one in five Missourians will be 65 or older by 2030.3

Families need an extra hand

Most older adults do not need a nurse. They need a ride to the pharmacy, a simple meal, the laundry done and someone to talk to. And the people who love them need to know that help is there, even on the weeks they cannot be.

Good people want work that matters

Every Missouri town is full of kind, dependable people: retirees, empty nesters, students, parents whose kids are in school. Many would love meaningful work that fits around their life, close to home, and pays fairly for their time.

Show-Me Neighbors brings them together. Families get freedom. Neighbors get flexible work that makes a real difference. And more of Missouri gets to grow older at home, with a friendly neighbor nearby.

Samantha, founder

1. Pew Research Center, August 2026. 2. AARP and the National Alliance for Caregiving, Caregiving in the U.S. 2025. 3. Missouri Department of Health and Senior Services, Missouri Master Plan on Aging.
