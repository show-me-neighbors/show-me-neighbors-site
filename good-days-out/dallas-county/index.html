Museums & historyBuffalo

Buffalo Head Prairie Historical Park and Dallas County Museum

See the county museum, an 1861 log cabin and an 1887 one-room school, with open house programs on county history.

Museum and library are wheelchair accessible

Research library 1st and 3rd Thursdays 10 to 2; open house 3rd Saturday March to November 10 to 4
