A good day out, anywhere in Missouri.

Lunch and cards at the senior center. A slow loop around the lake. Pie on the courthouse square, the county fair, a museum they have meant to see for years. Find real places near any Missouri town, then plan a day with a friendly local Neighbor who drives, walks alongside and keeps them company.

A week with something to look forward to
