Museums & historyRepublic

Wilson's Creek National Battlefield

Watch the museum film and exhibits in the visitor center, then drive the paved 4.9-mile one-way tour road with seven stops including the historic Ray House and Bloody Hill.

Visitor center and Ray House are wheelchair accessible from parking; a wheelchair is available in the visitor center; tour road stops are wheelchair accessible but trails off the road are primitive. Restrooms and water only at the visitor center.

Year-round.The park's 2026 Fees page says no entrance pass is required (an older tour road page mentions an entry fee; confirm at the visitor center).
nps.gov →

Parks & natureSpringfield

Mizumoto Japanese Stroll Garden

A 7.5-acre Japanese garden with meandering paths, a koi lake, moon bridge, tea house and meditation garden, created with sister city Isesaki, Japan.

The 0.51-mile main pathway is level stamped concrete with benches, suitable for wheelchairs; a few complimentary wheelchairs are available first come, and one motorized wheelchair can be rented for $15 for up to two hours (reserve at 417-891-1515); accessible parking near the entrance (path and wheelchair details per the Friends of the Garden Gardens Aglow page).

April through October; closed November through March except special events.$6 adults, $4 children 3 to 11.
