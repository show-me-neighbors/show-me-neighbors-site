Festivals & eventsKahoka

Clark County Mule Festival

A September tradition in the Missouri Mule Capital, with a mule and wagon parade, mule jumps and pulls, crafts, food and a Saturday rodeo.

Third full weekend in September$5 per person for the weekend; under 10 free
clarkcountymulefestival.com →

Clark County Parade of Champions

About 20 school marching bands parade through Kahoka in the afternoon, then perform field shows in the evening.

Oct. 10, 2026: parade 2 p.m., field show 5 p.m.
