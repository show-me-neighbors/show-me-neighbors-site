Safety and background checks

Trust comes first, before anyone rings the doorbell

Letting someone into Mom's home is a big deal. Here is exactly how we choose and check every Neighbor, before their first visit and every year after.

How we choose and check every Neighbor

Whether it is your home or your mom's, letting someone in is a big deal. Every Neighbor is chosen carefully, has a background check before their first visit, and has background checks again every year.

How we choose Neighbors

  • Application and screening questionsWe learn why they want to help and what they can do.
  • A one-on-one video interviewWe meet every Neighbor face to face on a live video call before they are approved.
  • Onboarding course and quizHow to be a good visitor, stay safe and spot when something is not right.

How we check them

  • Criminal background checkSearched across all 50 states.
  • Driving record checkBefore any Neighbor gives a ride.
  • Background checks every yearBackground checks and driving record checks are run again every year, not just once.
  • A visit note every timeNeighbors clock in and out in the app and leave a short note for the family.

Everyday help, never hands-on care

Neighbors help with company, rides, errands and light help around the house. They never give personal or medical care: no bathing, dressing, help in the bathroom, giving medication or helping someone walk, stand or move. And Neighbors never lift anyone, even after a fall. They call for help and stay with them.

A Neighbor never goes to an empty house. The person getting help is home for every visit, Neighbors check in and out in the app, and you get a short note after every visit.
